# Jeshtavakuppu Revision Quiz

Revision quiz for Grade 9 Sunday School (Jeshtavakuppu Part 1, CSI Madhya Kerala Diocese syllabus "Steps Towards Eternal Life").

Open the quiz: https://jithinkottaram.github.io/jeshtavakuppu-quiz/

Covers the ten exam lessons: God and Humankind; Be with God in Temptation; The Fruits of Obedience; The High Priestly Prayer of Jesus; The Holy Spirit: A Power or a Person?; The Vine and the Branches; God's Love and Human Love; Sin and Forgiveness; Journey Through the Epistles; Christian Stewardship.

Questions are drawn only from the children's workbook and the NIV Bible. The whole quiz is the single file `index.html` and also works offline when saved to a computer.
